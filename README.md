## Hello, I am Nicolas

Software Engineer. I like the part a lot of people skip: understanding the problem before writing the first line of code.

Nowadays I work on financial products at **[Finuse](https://finusepago.global)**. Most of my commits live in the company's private repositories, where I am the biggest individual contributor to a payment platform that has processed **over R$ 2 million** in transactions across Latin America and is in production on both the App Store and Google Play. This profile here is my lab, where I test personal ideas, build college projects and learn one step at a time.

### How I work

- **I start from the requirement, not from the code.** A lot of what I do starts with a conversation with someone who understands the business. I study the idea and, when I'm ready, I code.
- **I structure projects from the ground up.** Drawing the layers, deciding who has which responsibilities, paving the way for those who come after — it's the part that intrigues me the most and where I pour most of my energy.
- **I learn in public.** Almost every single one of my projects was a spontaneous idea that became a weekend of work.

I published a facial expression recognition model that received an **Outstanding Paper Award at COBICET 2024** (86.71% accuracy). I hold a bachelor's degree in Information Technology and I'm finishing another one in Software Engineering at the Federal Rural University of the Semi-Arid (UFERSA).
